Du’as for Protection

حِرْزٌ مِنَ الْأَذَى
A REFERENCE OF AUTHENTICATED SUPPLICATIONS — GRADED SAHIH OR HASAN
Nine well-attested du’as for protection, drawn only from narrations graded sahih (authentic) or hasan (good). Each entry gives the Arabic, transliteration, translation, occasion, a short explanation, and its full source.

Narrations within Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim carry consensus authenticity by virtue of inclusion in those two collections. For hadiths from the Sunan literature (Abu Dawud, al-Tirmidhi), the grading shown follows the assessments of the hadith specialist Muhammad Nasir al-Din al-Albani — principally Sahih Sunan Abi Dawud, Sahih Sunan al-Tirmidhi, and Sahih al-Jami‘ al-Saghir.

Prepared as a personal reference. Recitation of these du’as is a means of seeking Allah’s protection — it does not replace ordinary caution or lawful means of safety.
